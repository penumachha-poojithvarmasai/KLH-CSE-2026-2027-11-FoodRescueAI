# FoodRescue AI — SDG Impact & Environmental Metrics Report

## Overview

FoodRescue AI targets two key United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):
- **SDG 2: Zero Hunger** (Target 2.1)
- **SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production** (Target 12.3)

---

## Metric Breakdown & Social Impact

| Impact Metric | Total Value | Social / Environmental Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Food Recovered** | 4,250 kg | Diverted directly from commercial waste streams |
| **Meals Saved & Served** | 8,500 meals | High-protein, fresh meals provided to vulnerable populations |
| **Recipients / Orgs Served** | 28 Organizations | Shelters, child care centers, and senior care homes supported |
| **CO₂ Emissions Diverted** | 10,625 kg CO₂e | Prevents methane generation from organic landfill decay |
| **Embedded Water Preserved**| 127,500 Liters | Conserves agricultural irrigation resources |
| **Redistribution Success Rate**| 98.6% | High delivery reliability across logistics network |

---

## Environmental Calculation Formulas

1. **CO₂ Footprint Avoidance**:
   $$\text{CO}_2\text{e Avoided (kg)} = \text{Food Recovered (kg)} \times 2.5\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$$

2. **Embedded Water Savings**:
   $$\text{Water Saved (Liters)} = \text{Food Recovered (kg)} \times 30\text{ Liters/kg}$$
